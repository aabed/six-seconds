A psychological technology thriller in 24 short videos

Six Seconds

An engineer investigates a small attack on a small service and finds the clues point somewhere he did not want to look. Each chapter is one vertical video of about a minute and a half.

A work of fiction. Caldera, Relay, Current and all characters and events are invented. The story explores blame, institutional power and the limits of technical evidence; the investigation does not prove who committed the attack.

New chapters are added here as each video is finished.
